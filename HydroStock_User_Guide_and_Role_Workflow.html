1. What HydroStock Does

HydroStock manages the complete flow from plant spare-part inventory to purchase, billing, supplier delivery, and payment. It also stores plant equipment and maintenance information.

Material Need → Stock / Low Stock → Purchase Order → Approval → Supplier Dispatch → GST Invoice → Payment → Reports

5. Complete Demonstration Workflow

  1. Admin setup: Log in as Admin, review users, roles, equipment, and reports.
  2. Inventory setup: Log in as Store In-Charge, check the material catalog and low-stock alerts.
  3. Receive stock: Open Stock Inward, select material, enter positive quantity, challan number, and remarks, then submit.
  4. Issue stock: Open Stock Outward, select material, enter quantity and equipment/purpose. The system rejects a quantity greater than current balance.
  5. Create requirement: From low-stock results, the purchase user opens Create Purchase Order.
  6. Raise PO: Select supplier, set future delivery date, add material line items, quantity, unit price, and notes.
  7. Approve PO: The purchase order status progresses through PENDING, APPROVED, DISPATCHED, and DELIVERED.
  8. Supplier update: Supplier opens Purchase Orders and submits dispatch status with carrier/docket tracking notes.
  9. Generate invoice: Purchase user selects the PO, chooses GST type, verifies tax preview, and generates the invoice.
  10. Record payment: Accounts enters amount, mode, UTR/reference, and notes. The system updates UNPAID, PARTIALLY_PAID, or PAID.
  11. Review: Admin opens reports and dashboard to review the resulting operational totals.

7. Quick Troubleshooting Guide

ProblemCheck first
Login page appears repeatedlyConfirm the user is active and the correct role credentials are being used.
Database-backed lists are emptyStart MySQL, import database/database.sql, and verify database name hydrostock_db.
Application is unavailableStart Tomcat and run build_and_deploy.bat.
403 ForbiddenThe account is not authorized for that module; log in with the role responsible for it.
Stock outward rejectedReduce quantity to the current balance or below and provide a valid equipment/purpose remark.
PO cannot be submittedSelect a supplier, choose a future delivery date, add at least one item, and use quantity/price above zero.
Payment rejectedCheck the pending balance and provide a positive amount plus UTR/reference number.

8. Project Review Summary

A new reviewer can understand HydroStock by following the role order: Admin setup, Inventory requirement, Purchase order, Supplier delivery, GST invoice, Payment, and Reports. Every role has only the navigation needed for its responsibility, while the database relationships connect the work across modules.
